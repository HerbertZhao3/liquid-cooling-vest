# Chiller data request (Phase 2)

The Phase 2 design study needs real chiller data, not guesses. Send this request in week 2 to RIGID and to at least one other supplier. RIGID's addresses are ann@rigidhvac.com (store listing) and info@rigidhvac.com (datasheet).

If Phase 2 is ever built, buy only after a supplier has answered these questions in writing. Record each supplier's answers in the [decision log](decision-log.md) under D-001.

**Subject:** Data request: DV1920E-P(S) and personal-wear chiller for a university cooling-vest design

Hello,

I am a university student designing a battery-powered liquid-cooling vest. I am comparing your DV1920E-P(S) 24 V mini water chiller with the 2.75 kg, 260 W personal-wear system listed on your website, and I may buy one for a later build. Could you help with the following?

1. **Capacity map.** What are the cooling capacity and the electrical input power at 30 °C ambient air, with inlet water at 8, 12, 16, and 20 °C, at minimum and maximum compressor speed? A table for each unit would be ideal.
2. **Ambient limit.** Does the DV1920E-P(S) datasheet's "−16 to 30 °C" range refer to the evaporating temperature, the water temperature, or the ambient air? What is the maximum ambient temperature for continuous outdoor use? Is a "T" (tropical) version available for either unit?
3. **Water side.** What are the minimum and recommended water flows through the evaporator? What is the pressure drop at 0.5 L/min, and what size are the water connections?
4. **Scope of supply.** Is a condenser fan included, and if so, what are its voltage, power, and airflow? Is a pump included? Which temperature sensor does the controller require?
5. **Mass.** What does each unit weigh as supplied? The DV1920E-P(S) listing says 3.0 kg and its datasheet says 3.5 kg.
6. **Supply voltage.** The DV1920E-P(S) datasheet gives a 20–32 V range, but its troubleshooting table lists an 8.2 V under-voltage limit and a 17 V over-voltage limit. Which limits apply to the 24 V version, and what is the peak current at start-up?
7. **Restart.** After the compressor stops, how long must it wait before it can restart? What minimum run time and maximum number of starts per hour do you recommend?
8. **Tilt and falls.**
   - Does the 40° tilt limit apply while the unit is running?
   - Is a brief tilt of a few seconds acceptable?
   - After a tip-over, how long should the unit stand upright before it restarts?
   - Do you have any shock or drop rating, or recommended shock mounts?
9. **Speed control.** What signal voltage does the frequency (PWM) speed input need, and does it accept 3.3 V logic? Could you send the Modbus RTU register list?
10. **Freeze protection.** Does the controller protect the evaporator against freezing, or must the external system handle this?
11. **Commercial terms.** What are the price, shipping cost, and lead time to [city, country]? Are duties included (DDP)? Do you offer student or academic pricing?

Thank you,
[name]
